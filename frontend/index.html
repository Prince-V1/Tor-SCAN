Quick tips

Two simple additions to improve scan reliability.

01 · Better OCR

Use a straight, well-lit label photo

Avoid glare, extreme blur and tiny text. Keep the important declaration area inside the frame.

02 · Batch review

Scan similar products together

Batch scanning keeps each result separate while letting you review a group in one run.

03 · Evidence first

Check the detected text before acting

ScanMET is a screening prototype. Use the OCR evidence and field status as the basis for manual review.

About ScanMET

Simple, evidence-first label screening prototype.

OCR

Text extraction

ScanMET sends the selected label image to the configured backend OCR service and displays the returned evidence.

Rules

Field checks

Configured checks cover product identity and common packaged-label declarations such as MRP, quantity, dates, batch, manufacturer and consumer care.

History

Saved results

Each completed scan can be stored with its status, score, detected values and OCR evidence for later review.
